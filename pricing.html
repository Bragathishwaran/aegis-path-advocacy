FAQ

Pricing questions answered

Your monthly or annual flat fee covers all the services listed in your chosen plan with no hidden charges. Unlike hourly consulting, you'll never receive an unexpected invoice for a phone call or extra review session. We designed our pricing so families can plan ahead financially while receiving consistent, high-quality advocacy throughout the school year.

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), ACH bank transfer, and FSA/HSA cards where eligible. Payments are processed securely through Stripe. For annual plans, we also offer payment by check upon request.

Absolutely. You can upgrade your plan at any time and the change takes effect immediately — we'll pro-rate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. If your family's needs change throughout the year (for example, heading into a complex re-evaluation), upgrading temporarily to Comprehensive is always an option.

If within your first 30 days of service you're not satisfied for any reason, simply email us at hello@aegispointadvocacy.com with your name and a brief note. We'll process a full refund within 5–7 business days. No lengthy forms, no justification required — we stand behind our work completely.
