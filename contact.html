Flexible Advocacy Formats

Two Distinct Modes of Table Representation

Whether you need nationwide virtual consultations from the comfort of home or an experienced advocate seated beside you at the school meeting table, we have you covered.

Virtual Strategy & Meeting Representation

Connect securely from anywhere in the United States. We conduct live video strategy sessions, audit document drafts together on screen, and attend your school district's virtual IEP meetings.

  • Supported in all 50 states via secure video
  • Flexible morning, afternoon, and evening slots
  • Live advocate presence during online school meetings
Book Virtual Strategy Call

In-Person School Table Defense

For families within our regional Northeast service radius, an experienced advocate travels directly to your school to sit beside you at the table, ensuring firm, professional, non-adversarial representation.

  • Physical attendance at annual & triennial IEP meetings
  • Pre-meeting in-person strategy alignment briefing
  • Complete written post-meeting summary audit
