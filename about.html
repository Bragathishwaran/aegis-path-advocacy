Our Mission

We Believe Every Child Deserves a Champion

At AegisPoint Advocacy, our mission is to bridge the gap between what the law promises and what children with disabilities actually receive. We partner with families to ensure IEPs are ambitious, legally compliant, and faithfully implemented.

Founded in 2010 by a group of special education teachers, school psychologists, and parents of children with disabilities, AegisPoint has grown into one of the region's most trusted advocacy organizations—serving hundreds of families each year across the full spectrum of disabilities and school districts.

Our Core Values

Principles That Guide Our Work

Every decision we make, every meeting we attend, every document we prepare is grounded in these four commitments.

Family-Centered Partnership

We work alongside families as co-advocates—your knowledge of your child combined with our expertise creates the most powerful IEP team.

Transparent Communication

We translate dense legal and educational jargon into plain language so you always understand your rights and your child's options.

Data-Driven Results

Every recommendation we make is backed by assessment data, research on effective practices, and measurable progress indicators.

Unwavering Advocacy

We respectfully but firmly hold schools accountable to their legal obligations—because your child's future depends on an IEP that's actually implemented.

Our Journey

14 Years of Making a Difference

From a kitchen table idea to a full-service advocacy organization—here's how AegisPoint came to be.

2010

Founded in a Living Room

Dr. Maria Reyes and two colleagues begin informally helping local families prepare for IEP meetings after seeing children consistently denied appropriate services.

2012

Official 501(c)(3) Status

AegisPoint Advocacy achieves nonprofit status and opens its first office. First year serving over 60 families across three school districts.

2015

Parent Training Program Launched

Launched our signature "Know Your Rights" parent coaching curriculum, equipping families with the knowledge and tools to advocate independently.

2018

Statewide Expansion

Expanded services statewide with virtual advocacy. Team grew to 8 specialists covering all disability categories, from autism and ADHD to rare genetic conditions.

2021

AegisPoint Online Portal

Launched our secure parent portal, allowing families to upload IEP documents, schedule sessions, track case progress, and access resource libraries.

2024

1,200+ Families Served

We crossed the milestone of 1,200 families served, maintaining a 98% satisfaction rate and pioneering new bilingual advocacy services for Spanish-speaking communities.
