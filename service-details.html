Service Breakdown

What this service includes

An IEP is a legally binding contract, not an informal suggestion. Our review makes sure the plan on paper directly mirrors the individualized supports your child is guaranteed under IDEA.

We analyze the present IEP, recent neuropsychological or psychoeducational evaluations, standardized testing reports, and daily progress logs. We identify vague present levels, non-measurable goals, insufficient accommodation parameters, and procedural timeline violations. You walk away with a comprehensive written gap analysis, prioritized requests, and exact wording for your meeting agenda.

Deliverables

Complete Review Inclusions

  • Line-by-line audit of present levels, goals, accommodations & services
  • Compliance check against IDEA mandates & state-specific regulations
  • Written gap analysis highlighting missing evaluations and services
  • Custom SMART goals drafting with objective measurement baselines
  • Structured 1-page parent agenda & scripted meeting talking points
  • 60-minute strategy video consultation with your primary advocate

The 4-Step Process

How We Review & Elevate the IEP

  1. 1.Secure Document Intake

    Upload your child's draft IEP, prior testing, report cards, and behavioral observations through our HIPAA/FERPA-compliant parent portal.

  2. 2.Comprehensive Legal & Clinical Audit

    Our multidisciplinary team (former school psychologists and special educators) audits every section for measurable baselines and IDEA fidelity.

  3. 3.Written Plan & Strategy Consultation

    You receive our complete written audit report within 5 business days, followed by a 60-minute call to rehearse parent requests and questions.

  4. 4.Meeting Attendance & Post-Review Follow-up

    We attend your school meeting (virtually or in-person) to ensure agreements are officially memorialized in the Prior Written Notice (PWN).
