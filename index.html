The process

A clear path from confusion to confidence

Most families feel lost in paperwork. We replace that with four simple, supported steps.

  1. 1.Free intake call

    We listen to your story, review your documents and identify urgent concerns before any commitment.

  2. 2.Records review

    We audit the IEP, evaluations and progress data against IDEA requirements and identify gaps.

  3. 3.Strategy & preparation

    You receive a meeting agenda, talking points and proposed goals in writing — before you walk in.

  4. 4.Meeting & follow-up

    We advocate with you, then confirm every agreement in a written summary you can rely on.
